About Pico Picks

Driven By Passion For Cars

Pico Picks is Bangladesh's premier trusted importer of high-detail die-cast model cars, collectibles, and automotive miniatures. Founded by dedicated car enthusiasts, our goal is to make the pleasure of collecting accessible to collectors of all ages.

We bring authentic global goods directly to your doorstep with guaranteed quality assurance, affordable value, and responsive customer care.

  • 100% Authentic & Officially Licensed Models
  • Transparent & Affordable Pricing
  • Multi-Layer Protective Packaging & Fast Shipping
  • Dedicated Customer Support & Satisfaction Guarantee
