Return & Refund Policy

We want you to love every miniature masterpiece in your collection. If something isn't right with your order, our dedicated customer support team is here to assist you.

7-Day Replacement Guarantee

All diecast cars, scale miniatures, and RC vehicles purchased through Pico Picks are covered under our 7-Day Replacement Guarantee starting from the date your package is delivered.

You are eligible for an immediate replacement, store credit, or full refund if your item:

  • Arrives physically damaged during transit or shipping.
  • Contains a proven manufacturing defect (e.g., misaligned axle, non-functioning RC electronics, missing parts).
  • Differs noticeably from the model, scale, or color ordered.

How the Return Process Works

We keep the return procedure simple, fast, and transparent across all districts in Bangladesh:

1

Submit Request

Contact our support team within 7 days with your Order ID and clear photos/unboxing video showing the issue.

2

Quick Review

Our claims department verifies the condition within 24 to 48 hours and issues return authorization.

3

Courier Pickup

Our courier partner will collect the item from your doorstep or you can send it to our hub address.

4

Refund or Swap

Receive an identical brand-new replacement unit or a full refund via your preferred payment method.

Eligibility Criteria

To qualify for a smooth return or exchange, please ensure the following criteria are met:

  • The item must be in its original collector box with factory plastic blisters, straps, and protective foam intact.
  • All included accessories (screws, plinths, display bases, remote controls, antennas) must be returned together.
  • Damage resulting from accidental user drops, intentional disassembly, or misuse is not covered under the guarantee.
  • Unboxing video documentation is strongly recommended for transit damage claims to expedite carrier processing.

Refund Timelines & Methods

Once your returned parcel is received and inspected at our quality warehouse:

  • bKash / Nagad: Refunds are disbursed within 24 to 72 business hours.
  • Bank Transfer: Processed within 3 to 5 working days depending on your bank's clearance.
  • Store Voucher: Instantly credited with an extra promotional coupon bonus for future purchases.

Our friendly customer support specialists are ready to answer your questions and assist with any return or exchange requests.
